The process

From an open book to a clear number.

Three moving parts, working quietly in the background of every study session.

01 — CAPTURE

Log the session

Start a timer, pick a subject and topic. Duration, breaks and focus quality get captured without extra effort on your part.

02 — PROCESS

Structure the data

Sessions are organised into subjects, topics and weeks automatically, building a running history you can actually read.

03 — DIRECT

Act on the analysis

The dashboard surfaces what's falling behind and what's working, so next week's plan is a decision, not a guess.
