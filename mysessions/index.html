Why MySession

Built for real focus

Video on, distractions off
Live video with your room keeps you present. Seeing others working is the simplest accountability system that exists.
24/7 rooms, no waiting
Drop in at 2am or 6am — there's always a room open with people working. No scheduled start time, no waiting around.
ADHD-friendly structure
Timed blocks, visible task lists, and the gentle pressure of others watching means you actually start — and finish.
Buddy sessions of 3
A tight circle of three people with screenshare. Great for recurring sessions and building habits that actually stick.
Celebrate wins
Every session ends with a win log. Share what you finished, see what others accomplished, leave feeling capable.
No pressure to talk
Mic optional. Camera optional but encouraged. You don't need to perform — just show up and work alongside everyone else.
