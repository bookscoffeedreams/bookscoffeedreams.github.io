Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Plain-language summary of how we handle your information. Please read it alongside our Terms of Service.

1. What this policy covers

Books Coffee & Dreams (“we”, “us”) runs a community and learning platform with courses, tests, study tools, blogs and a community space. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the site, why, and what choices you have.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address and profile photo, handled through Firebase Authentication using the sign-in method you choose.
  • Learning data — courses you enrol in, lesson and test progress, study plans, XP and streaks, and any notes or workspace pages you create in our tools.
  • Community content — posts, chat messages, files you upload and basic presence information (such as whether you are online).
  • Purchases — a record of the courses you buy. Payments are processed by Razorpay; we do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Staff applications — if you apply to join the team, everything you enter in the application form (for example your name, age, Discord tag, email, country, experience and availability).
  • On your device — preferences such as light/dark mode and unfinished application drafts are saved in your browser’s local storage.

3. How we use it

  • to create and secure your account and show your progress;
  • to deliver courses, tests and tools you have access to;
  • to process purchases and give you access to what you bought;
  • to run and moderate the community and keep it safe;
  • to review staff applications and contact applicants;
  • to maintain and improve the platform.

4. Services we rely on

We use trusted providers to run the platform: Google Firebase (authentication and database), Razorpay (payments), Cloudinary (image and file hosting), Discord (our community server) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). They process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your display name, photo and anything you post in community areas can be seen by other members. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the safety of our members and platform.

6. Storage and security

Your data is stored with the providers above. We use reasonable measures to protect it, but no online service can be completely secure, so please use a strong password and keep your sign-in details private.

7. Your choices

You can view and update your details from your Profile and Settings pages. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account, contact us using the link below and we will help. Some records (for example purchase history) may need to be kept where we have a legitimate or legal reason to do so.

8. Younger users

If you are under 18, please use the platform with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform changes. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed; continuing to use the site after an update means you accept the new version.

Questions about this page? Reach us in the Books Coffee & Dreams Discord community and ask a member of the team.
